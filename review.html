These are the ads in the app. Each place can have several offers: a club-specific one beats the general one, and so do home/away, match-day and league ones. An offer only shows when it's switched On, and a place is never left empty: with none of its own switched on, it borrows another live ad (footer tiles from the other tiles, the deals card and score-card ad from the kit ads), and if there's none at all the app shows its own built-in ad with no link. Changes reach fans the next time they open the app.
Text can use {team} the fan's team, {opponent}, {venue}, {home} {away}, {side} home/away, {when} Today or the day, {weekday}, {night} tonight or e.g. Sat night. The preview fills them in for Leeds Utd v Tottenham (an away game for a Spurs fan, Saturday).

Anonymous counts from the app: no names, accounts or device IDs. A "fan" is one phone that opened the app that day (or week, or month). Downloads, uninstalls, ratings and crashes are in Google Play Console. UK dates.
